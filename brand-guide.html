Atlas Dance

Dexter Ellis | Utah Ballroom Studio | March 2026

Brand Concept

Atlas Dance is a private ballroom studio led by Dexter Ellis in Utah, coaching high school dancers toward national competition. The brand identity is built on the tension between strength and grace — the mythological Atlas bearing the weight of the world, expressed through the elegance of dance. This duality drives every visual choice: monumental type paired with fluid serifs, dark grounding tones lifted by gold accents, and a tone that is demanding but deeply supportive.

Logo Typography

The wordmark pairs two typefaces: Cinzel for "Atlas" (strength, structure, permanence) and Cormorant Garamond Italic for "Dance" (grace, movement, fluidity). The two words should always appear together and be treated as a single visual block. "Dance" renders in gold to accent the primary ivory/white of "Atlas."

Brand Voice

Atlas Dance speaks with quiet authority. The tone is literary, not corporate — poetic but never precious. Sentences are short and declarative. The writing respects the reader's intelligence and never oversells. Key themes:

Confidence"The floor is waiting.""We're the best studio around!"
Intimacy"Your heart goes down first.""Our students love us!"
High Standards"He won't go easy on you. That's the point.""We push you to be your best self!"
Story-Driven"We don't train bodies. We train storytellers.""Learn all five ballroom styles!"
